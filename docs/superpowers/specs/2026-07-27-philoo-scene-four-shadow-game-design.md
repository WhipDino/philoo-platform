# Philoo Scene Four: The Shadow Game Design

## Scope

Replace only `/aula/as-sombras/eles-dao-nomes`, the story scene immediately
after the cave descent. Keep the following activity at
`/aula/as-sombras/o-que-chegou-ate-eles` unchanged so its redesign can be
discussed separately.

## Purpose

The learner should witness how the prisoners turn recurring appearances into
names, status, and apparent knowledge. The scene must make one distinction
memorable before the activity begins:

> Recognizing an appearance is not the same as knowing what produces it.

## Story

The learner arrives just behind the prisoners. Platão speaks quietly and draws
attention to the wall. A bird-shaped shadow crosses it, and one prisoner
celebrates recognizing it first. Platão interprets that small triumph without
revealing the hidden fire, objects, or people that create the shadows.

The scene uses four sequential dialogue beats:

1. **Platão:** “Agora, olhe com elas. Tudo o que conseguem ver acontece nesta
   parede.”
2. **Prisioneiro:** “Um pássaro! Eu reconheci primeiro!”
3. **Platão:** “Aqui, quem reconhece as sombras mais depressa parece ser o mais
   sábio.”
4. **Platão:** “Eles conhecem muito bem as aparências — mas nunca viram o que
   as produz. Guarde essa diferença.”

Final action: **Observar as sombras**

## Visual composition

Create dedicated desktop and mobile illustrations rather than reusing or
cropping `cave-recurring-shadows-v1.webp`.

### Desktop

- 16:9 cinematic composition.
- Camera sits just behind and slightly above three seated prisoners.
- Platão is integrated into the lower-left foreground, in three-quarter
  profile, directing the learner toward the wall.
- The three prisoners occupy the lower-middle and face away from the learner.
- A warm wall fills the right half, with softly projected bird, amphora, and
  horse shadows.
- Dark cave rock at the upper-left remains quiet for the dialogue interface.
- Platão's lamp lights him and nearby stone but is visibly not the source of
  the large wall shadows.

### Mobile

- Native 9:16 composition, not a desktop crop.
- Quiet dark rock fills the upper 38% for the scene label and longest dialogue.
- The shadow wall occupies the middle.
- Platão remains a principal character in the lower-left.
- The prisoners remain visible from behind in the lower-right.

The hidden fire, source objects, and puppeteers must not be revealed yet.

## Interface

Use the approved Scene 3 voice-card language:

- cream rounded voice card;
- large opening quotation mark;
- small speaker attribution;
- sequential progress and one action;
- no triangle, tail, headline, narration panel, name tags, or miniature
  separate Platão.

Platão's dialogue uses the Philoo baby-blue quote signature. The prisoner's
single line uses a restrained warm-amber quote signature, while preserving the
same card shape so it remains visibly dialogue.

## Motion and continuity

Scene 3 moves forward toward the distant prisoners. Scene 4 enters as a closer
over-the-prisoners view, with the wall settling into focus before the first
voice card appears. Dialogue changes use the same short, reduced-motion-safe
card transition as Scene 3.

## Responsive and accessibility requirements

- Preserve the existing header, `4 of 10` progress, back route, next route,
  and shared scene transition.
- Provide separate descriptive text for the desktop and mobile artwork.
- Decorative quotation marks remain hidden from assistive technology.
- Announce each dialogue beat through the existing polite live region.
- Keep every action keyboard accessible and maintain visible focus.
- Neither artwork may place important faces, gestures, prisoners, or shadows
  beneath the dialogue card.

## Acceptance criteria

- The scene feels like an observed story moment rather than a lesson slide.
- Platão, the prisoners, and the shadows belong to one coherent illustration.
- The learner sees a prisoner name a shadow before Platão interprets it.
- All four beats remain readable at desktop and phone widths.
- The wall, prisoners, and Platão remain visible throughout.
- The current activity source, styles, tests, and route remain untouched.
